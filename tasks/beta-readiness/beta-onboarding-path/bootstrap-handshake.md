# Twilite

You are going to put someone's work into Twilite, a workspace where work is kept as a graph of cards. Read the guide first; it has everything you need, including working examples:

- `https://twilite.zone/library/documentation/OnboardLLM.md`

If you cannot open links, say so and ask the person to paste the guide.

Then answer with one fenced `json` block and nothing around it: a Twilite `transaction`, as the guide shows. The person will paste your reply into Twilite.

Here is what they want:

> I want to keep track of ____ . Use goals, tasks, and questions.
